■ App name (max 30)
Oshiuma Note: JRA Race Alerts

■ Short description (max 80)
Follow your favorite JRA horses: alerts when entered, before post time & results

■ Full description (max 4000)
When does your favorite horse run next?
No more searching the race cards every week.
Star a horse and Oshiuma Note tells you when it's entered, 30 minutes before post time, and as soon as the result is official.

Works right after install. No sign-in needed.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Alerts that keep you in the loop
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
・Entry: when your horse is entered in a race (also for horses you've taken notes on)
・Race day morning: a digest of your horses running today
・30 minutes before post: with the current win odds and popularity
・Right after the race: the official finishing position
・Graded races: the top favorites and odds 30 minutes before post (can be turned off)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Find horses to follow
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
・Search by name and tap ☆ to follow
・Not sure who to pick? Choose from this week's graded race runners or the most-followed horses
・Your next runner appears big at the top of the home screen, with the rest lined up beside it

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Make each horse's card your own
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
・Pick an icon (🏆 👑 🍀 🌸 and more) and a background color for every horse you follow
・When your horse wins, share it on X, LINE and more with one tap

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Horse profiles
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
・Next race and full race history on one screen
・Records by turf/dirt, distance and racecourse to spot each horse's strengths
・Keep notes and look back at them before the next race

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Make picks and check them
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
・Mark ◎○▲△ on the race card and see how your picks did after the race
・Win, top-2 and top-3 rates of your ◎ picks are tallied automatically
・Record bets to track your return from the official payouts (record only — no betting in the app)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Everything for race day
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
・Race cards with each runner's recent form and hints such as layoffs, distance changes and jockey changes
・Win and place odds, body weight, scratches, jockey changes, weather and track condition
・Full results with draw colors and popularity, and payouts by bet type
・Main race results from every racecourse on the home screen, G1 first
・Course trends: upset rates, sires, jockeys and trainers by racecourse

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Good to know
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
・Free to use (shows ads)
・Race cards show the last 3 runs; watch an ad to see the last 10 for the rest of the day
・No sign-in needed. Sign in with Google to keep your horses, notes and picks when you change phones
・Requires an internet connection to load race cards, odds and results
・The app is available in English, but horse names, race names and jockey names are shown in Japanese as published

※ Covers JRA (Japan Racing Association) races. Results are available from October 2025 onward.
※ You can't buy betting tickets in this app.
※ We do our best to keep the information accurate, but it is not guaranteed. Please check the official announcements for final results and odds.
